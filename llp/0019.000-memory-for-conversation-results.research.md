## Summary

The four new parts (LLP 0019#the-new-parts) on round 11's benchmark: 18 people's real conversations and 392
re-told questions, OpenAI only, aggregates only.
- **Meaning search is the biggest single gain.** Ranking the conversation by embeddings instead of words recalls
  67.5%, against 57.4% (+10.1, 95% CI 7.3 to 13.1). On facts first said in Czech the gain is +23.9.
- **Statements are the unit.** Lasting statements extracted from each conversation, searched by meaning, recall
  66.2% with 1,112 input tokens per question: a 34th of what reading the whole history uses.
- **The three parts together match reading everything.** Dossier, statements and the conversation searched by
  words and meaning recall 70.3%, against 70.0% (+0.3, CI -1.9 to 2.6), at a quarter of the tokens.
- **Any two of the three do as well** (69.1-71.4%, exploratory). Dossier + statements alone recall 71.4% at 5,581
  tokens per question.
- **The dossier alone falls 6.2 points short of reading everything,** at an eighth of the tokens. It still recalls
  24 points more than what the product's memory injects today.
- **Building costs $0.26 for all 18 accounts:** 4,376 statements and 18 dossiers. The round cost about $3.55.
- **Repeat counts didn't work.** At the pre-set merge threshold, 36 of 4,376 statements merged: with this embedding
  model, paraphrases rarely reach a cosine of 0.88.

- **Meaning search fixes the language gap.** Round 10's weakness on facts first said in Czech came from word search:
  an English question shares no words with a Czech sentence. Embeddings place both near each other (cosine 0.58 for a
  sentence and its translation, 0.20 for an unrelated one).
- **Fusing words with meaning is slightly worse than meaning alone** (65.7 against 67.5). In conversation, the
  question's words rarely match the person's.

- **The parts overlap.** Any two of the three recall about as much as all three, and as much as reading the whole
  history.
- **Removing any one changes nothing we can measure.**
- **The cheapest near-equal is statements plus a dossier,** at a seventh of the tokens of reading everything.
  Statements with meaning search come within 1.1 points at a tenth (-1.1, CI -3.7 to 1.4).

## Building

- **Statements:**
  - 4,376 over the 18 accounts, 85-663 per account, from one LLM call per window.
  - With 18 dossiers and the embeddings, building cost $0.26 in all.
- **Merging paraphrases didn't happen.**
  - 36 of 4,376 statements merged at the pre-set cosine 0.88.
  - On one account, no statement's nearest neighbour passed 0.72.
  - So the store has no repeat counts. A merge that asks the model "same fact?" of the nearest candidates is the
    fix (next).
- **Dossiers:** 10k-23k characters each, from one read of up to 335k characters.

## Hypotheses

LLP 0019#hypotheses, pooled over the 18 accounts.
- **H1 (meaning beats words, +5; +10 on Czech): supported.** +8.3 (+10.1 for meaning alone); +23.9 on Czech.
- **H2 (statements ≥ facts + log): supported.** +7.8, at a quarter of the tokens.
- **H3 (the dossier within 5 points of the whole history, at a fifth of the tokens): not supported on accuracy**
  (-6.2). Supported on tokens (an eighth).
- **H4 (together ≥ 65%, and facts + log + 7): supported.** 70.3%, +11.9.

## What this changes (LLP 0015)

- **For conversation, the memory is three parts over the raw log** [inferred: one benchmark, one judge]:
  - statements about the person, extracted per conversation
  - a dossier from one full read, refreshed in the background
  - the raw conversation, kept, found by meaning rather than words
- **Any two parts recall as much as reading the whole history** at a quarter to a seventh of the tokens, and beyond
  the context window they still work.
- **Search by meaning.** Word search was the weak point of every earlier memory in this lab on conversation, and of
  the product's recall. Embeddings cost $0.02 per million tokens.
- **The facts tables stay for what conversation doesn't carry:** exact counts, sums and state (LLP 0016.000). For
  therapy talk they held little.
- **For the product,** the dossier alone is a nightly job that would lift what its prompt gets from 40% to 64% on
  these questions. Statements with meaning search would lift it to 66-69%, with 1-4k-token prompts against its 1.8k
  (LLP 0018.000).

## Deviations

- **A smoke test ran on one account (u27) before the full run,** with the same configs. Nothing was changed after it.
- **The merge threshold stayed at 0.88** although the smoke test showed it would merge almost nothing, as the RFC
  fixed it before the run.
- **The combination lesions and the lean pair are exploratory,** added after the main results.
- **The judge is the OpenAI shim** (80.5% exact agreement with Jev on round 10's answers, LLP 0018.000).
- **The statement store's prompt is worded differently from the gold's extractor,** but both write third-person
  statements. The match may be easier for the judge.
