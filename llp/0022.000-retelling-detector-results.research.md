## Summary

- **The detector doubles how often the assistant acknowledges the exact fact a person is repeating:** 22.0%,
  against 10.2% with the directive prompt alone (+11.9, 95% CI 8.1 to 15.9), with no invented memories (0 of 54).
- **When it fires, the assistant acknowledges the fact 39.5% of the time.**
- **As a classifier it is noisy.**
  - On other people's data (the OpenAI model in Jev's place), it fires on 42% of re-tellings, and 13.2% of its
    picks trace back to the verified earlier mention.
  - It raises a false alarm on 30.5% of turns where everything said was new.
- **Real Jev, on the maintainer's data, detects twice as many** (26.2% against 12.3%), with more false alarms (21.0%
  against 12.4%), in 0.32 s against 3.3 s.
- **The round cost $0.60.**

- **"Picks the earlier mention" undercounts.** The verified earlier mentions are at most three windows. A pick of
  the same fact said in another window counts as a miss. The conversation effect is larger than the detection
  numbers would suggest.

## Hypotheses

- **D1 (detection ≥ 50%, false alarms ≤ 20%): not supported.** 13.2% and 30.5% on the 18 accounts; 26.2% and 21.0%
  with real Jev.
- **D2 (strict same fact ≥ 30%, invents ≤ 5%): not supported on the rate** (22.0%, a doubling). Supported on
  invention (0 of 54).
- **D3 (real Jev within 5 points of the OpenAI model, at under 1 s): supported, and more.** Jev detects +13.9 more,
  with +8.6 more false alarms, at a tenth of the time.

## What this changes (LLP 0015, LLP 0023)

- **The detector belongs in the turn loop.** It is the one step that moved the conversation, not just the recall.
- **Its threshold is the precision knob.** Real Jev's calibrated probabilities make it tunable; 0.5 fires often.
- **The package (LLP 0023) exposes it as a separate step** with its own threshold, so a product can tune it on its
  own traffic.

## Deviations

- **The false-alarm controls are the turns where the gold found only new lasting statements.** Some may still
  repeat something the gold didn't list.
