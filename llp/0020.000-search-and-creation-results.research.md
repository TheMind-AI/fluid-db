## Summary

Round 13, on LLP 0018.000's benchmark: 18 people's real conversations, 392 re-told statements, OpenAI only,
aggregates only. The maintainer's own data adds a real-Jev comparison.
- **The best memory so far:** the incremental dossier, merged statements and windows, the last two picked by Jev
  (`conv_best`).
  - It recalls **72.1%** of what people re-told, against 70.0% for reading their whole history (+2.0, CI -0.3 to
    4.3).
  - It uses a quarter of the tokens.
- **Search, replayed at every turn and scored without a judge:**
  - Meaning beats words at every moment (+9 to +13 points of hit@10).
  - Reranking the top 20 is the biggest gain: +7.4 hit@5 on windows, +4.0 hit@10 on statements.
  - Query rewriting, context headers and word-and-meaning fusion don't help.
- **The memory can anticipate.** Before the person says it, it surfaces the earlier mention 82% as often as after
  (66.1% against 81.1% hit@10).
- **Jev should pick the evidence, not the search.**
  - A router that picks the search from the question never beat the best single search (-1.5).
  - A picker that chooses among found candidates gained +6.1 over the best single search.
  - Routing stores end to end cost 3.2 points and more tokens.
- **On the maintainer's own data, real Jev picks as well as the OpenAI model** (+1.0, CI -2.9 to 4.8), in 0.33 s
  against 2.1 s per decision.
- **Creation:**
  - The model-confirmed merge finds the repeats: 46% of statements belong to something said more than once, and it
    records 278 changes. Keeping one wording per merged fact loses detail (-3.3 end to end).
  - A dossier kept current in batches is as good as one full read (-1.0).
- **The round cost $5.20** (the runs' own ledgers), plus $0.03 of Jev on the maintainer's data.

  window's context helps.

## Part B: creation

- **The model-confirmed merge:**
  - 4,426 statements became 3,060 items; 668 of them were said more than once, up to 24 times.
  - 46.0% of all statements sit in repeated items (25-60% per account), and 278 changes were recorded.
  - Cosine alone had merged under 1% (LLP 0019.000).
- **The incremental dossier:**
  - Updated after every 10 windows: 3-15 updates per account.
  - It came out longer than the one-read dossier (6,068 against 4,743 tokens) and recalls as much (below).

- **The contrasts:**

  - `conv_best` - `conv_all`: +1.8 (-0.3 to 3.8)

- **Why merging lost detail:** a merged fact keeps its latest wording, and earlier wordings held specifics the
  question asked for [inferred]. The counts are worth keeping. The single wording is not.

## Part D: Jev picks the search

`deprecated/python/lab/bench/replay.py route` and `route_summary`. hit@5 over windows.

**18 accounts, 1,176 moments (the OpenAI model in Jev's place):**

| strategy | cue | reactive | proactive | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| words | 51.0 | 61.0 | 44.6 | 52.2 |
| meaning | 63.8 | 65.3 | 51.8 | 60.3 |
| words and meaning (the best single) | 64.3 | 66.8 | 53.1 | 61.4 |
| **router:** one search, picked from the moment | 65.1 | 63.0 | 51.5 | 59.9 |
| router, two picks fused | 64.5 | 64.8 | 54.3 | 61.2 |
| **picker:** all five searches pooled, the best 5 picked | **70.9** | **75.3** | **56.4** | **67.5** |
| the pool holds the mention (the picker's ceiling) | 83.2 | 86.7 | 71.4 | 80.4 |

- **The contrasts:**
  - router - best single: -1.5 (-4.3 to 1.2)
  - picker - router: +7.7 (4.8 to 10.4)
  - picker - best single: +6.1 (3.7 to 8.7)
- **Pooling five searches adds nothing over one good search:** the picker (67.5%) equals reranking the meaning top
  20 (67.7%, Part A). The gain is in the picking. The ceiling of 80% says a better picker could still gain 13
  points.
- **The router mostly chose rewriting (631) or meaning (516),** and never words, although words alone found 12.4%
  of what meaning missed. From the moment alone it can't tell which search will work.

**The maintainer's own data, 207 moments, real Jev and the OpenAI model:**

| | Jev | OpenAI model |
|---|---|---|
| best single search (meaning) | 51.7 | 51.7 |
| router | 50.7 | 49.8 |
| picker | **63.8** | 62.8 |
| time per decision: routing / picking | **0.33 s / 0.34 s** | 1.51 s / 2.13 s |

- **Jev picker minus the OpenAI picker: +1.0 (-2.9 to 4.8).** The two routers agree on the search in 87 of 207
  moments.

## Hypotheses

LLP 0020#hypotheses and #part-d-jev-picks-the-search.
- **R1 (meaning ≥ words + 10, cue and proactive): half.** Cue +12.8 (7.4 to 18.1); proactive +9.4 (4.1 to 14.8).
- **R2 (rerank + 5 at 5 windows): supported.** +7.4.
- **R3 (rewriting + 3): not supported.** -1.0.
- **R4 (headers + 3): not supported.** -2.4.
- **R5 (proactive ≤ 0.6 × reactive): not supported.** 0.82: anticipation is easier than expected. The assistant's
  question usually sets up the topic.
- **R6 (statements at 25 ≥ windows at 5, at a quarter of the characters): not supported** (-3.8), though it holds
  for cue questions.
- **C1 (≥ 15% of statements repeated): supported.** 46.0%.
- **C2 (merging costs ≤ 1 point): not supported.** -3.3.
- **C3 (the kept-current dossier within 3 of one read): supported.** -1.0.
- **E1 (`conv_best` ≥ the best of `conv_all` and the whole history - 1, at ≤ a quarter of the tokens):
  supported.** 72.1%, at 24% of the tokens.
- **D1 (picker ≥ rerank + 5): not supported.** Equal.
- **D2 (router ≥ best single + 3): not supported.** -1.5.
- **D3 (picker ≥ router + 5): supported.** +7.7.
- **D4 (Jev within 3 of the OpenAI model, at ≤ a fifth of the latency): supported.** +1.0, at a sixth.
- **D5 (routing stores end to end ≥ `conv_best` - 1): not supported.** -3.2, with more tokens.

## What this changes (LLP 0015)

- **The best way for conversation, as measured:**
  - **Write:**
    - the raw log
    - statements per window, embedded; keep every wording, and link repeats with counts instead of replacing them
    - a dossier updated after each session
    - typed tables only for what must be counted
  - **Read:**
    - the dossier at the start of a chat
    - at every turn, meaning search over statements and windows (top 20 each); Jev picks the evidence to inject
    - SQL for counts
- **Jev's place in reading is the picker, not the router.** Choosing among candidates it can see is the closed
  decision Jev is good at. It is as accurate as an LLM here, and fast enough for every turn (0.33 s). Guessing which
  search or store will work, from the question alone, doesn't beat always using the best one. On conversation it
  cost points and tokens.
- **Don't add query rewriting or context headers** to this pipeline: they cost calls and didn't help.
- **The picker's ceiling (80%)** says better picking, not more searching, is where the next points are.

## Deviations

- **Part D was added after Part A's results,** with its hypotheses written before it ran.
- **`route_summary`'s account guard read the mode argument as an account and refused.** It was fixed before any
  summary was computed. No data was affected.
- **Two tests had identical discordant counts (77 against 40).** They were checked: different statements are
  behind them, a coincidence.
- **The judge is the OpenAI shim** (80.5% exact agreement with Jev, LLP 0018.000). Part A and Part D use no judge.
