## Question

How do writes and reads work at 5,000+ memories, and what is the best setup? [confirmed] (Adam Zvada, 2026-09-25,
paraphrased from a dictated message): "how would read and write work in like 5000+ memories?", generating the data
and spending at most about $3. Details: `deprecated/python/lab/REPORT.md` §23-25.

## Setup

- **Data:** `simulate_scale.py`, 5,050 messages over 2.5 years with per-message truth on 4,537, and 72 questions.
- **The tiered writer:**
  - the first 400 messages go through the planner
  - regex patterns are then compiled test-driven from the planner's own rows and applied by code
  - the Jev gate and the planner handle the rest
  - patterns are recompiled every 500 messages
- **Reads on the result:** the deterministic reader, the Luna agent, and the hybrid.

## Results

| finding | numbers | § |
|---|---|---|
| Code writes most messages | 70% overall, 77-85% in steady state, ~1 ms | §24 |
| Code writes are as accurate | 95.5% right vs truth; on the same kinds as the planner, e.g. expenses 92.3% vs 92.2% | §24 |
| Cost | $0.85 fresh vs ~$3.50 all-LLM | §24 |
| Drift | the move to San Francisco cut code's share to 33% until the next recompile | §24 |
| Wrong writes (235 of 4,537) | 106 late-night dates; 60 merchant spellings (28 accents); 52 companions (17 from one wrong nickname); 16 no row | §24 |
| Reads | agent 80.6% (3.7 s); deterministic alone 66.7% (0.7-0.8 s); hybrid at 0.7: 81.9%, 64% answered without an LLM | §25 |
| The weak point | filter choice over messy columns (lunch, dinner and drinks inside `dining` + description) | §25 |

## What we learned

- **The LLM as a compiler works for writes too.** Test the patterns against the model's own rows, and send the
  failures back.
- **Recompile on drift,** not on a schedule.
- **The verifier threshold must be stricter at scale:** 0.7, not 0.5.
- **The remaining errors are schema hygiene,** which led to round 5 (LLP 0003).

## Decisions

- The tiered writer (LLP 0005#compiled-writer) and a hybrid threshold of 0.7 (LLP 0006#hybrid).

## Caveats

- **The threshold was chosen on the evaluated questions.**
- **Parallel runs misattributed per-question cost.** It was recomputed per tag.
